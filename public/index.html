Welcome to BurnHalla


First things first: this is not your regular burn night. This is a full 24-hour BurnHalla experience!


So what does that mean?


  • The program with workshops, performances and shows starts at 12:00 noon — don’t miss out & come early!
  • There is nothing for sale on site!
  • BurnHalla bars run entirely on collective donations! Bring something with you to the event or contribute ahead of time to the drinks donation pool!
  • Don’t forget to bring your own cup!
  • If you want to eat, bring a plate and something for the potluck.

More Info

Programme


We got a stacked programme with everything from workshops, to cabaret, to high line performances, fire shows, dance floors and so much more.


The full schedule will be published here soon. Make sure to arrive early! We start at noon.

Food & Drinks


BurnHalla is a non-commercial burner experience which means nothing will be for sale on site.


That also means that bars and food depend on your donations.


You can either bring drinks to site or donate to the money pool here.

The Space


For 24 hours you can explore BurnHalla on 3 different levels with astonishing art, diverse dance floors, themed bars, workshop rooms, cozy and sexy spaces, high-flying performances, unexpected disco shapes, light shows, and more.


Find the map here.


How to contribute?


We will turn a bare machine hall with dusty basements into a miraculous wonderland only because each and everyone does their part. Here are some suggestions on ways to contribute.

🎨 Decorate a Space

Some spaces are still waiting for magic. If you want to bring lights, blankets, hippie traps, any object, or visual chaos, please do. Let us know via this form so we can coordinate and support you. Tagging machines are available.


🍸 Support the bars

Donate to the shared money pool before the event or bring a bottle contribution on site. There are no drinks included in the ticket price, and bars depend entirely on your donations. Spread the word!


🍕 Bring a food dream

Pizza oven, paella, cheese fondue, midnight soup: food is love and an incredible way to gift something special to the community. Sign up on the sheet.


🙋 Volunteer

There are still a few volunteer shifts left and we especially need help with transport on Thursday and Monday. Sign up here.

👗 Dress accordingly and get lost in layers

Some areas will feel like outdoor temperatures, others will be heated. Layers are your friend. So bring your burner coat with fairy lights for the main hall and house shoes or thick socks for shoes-off spaces.There will be multiple wardrobes to help us keep things in order.


🏷️ Label your stuff >> Leave no trace >> Lost & Found

In that order! We will reiterate this again. And again. Aaaaand. Again. Watch out for your things. Label them. We will be over a thousand people, and we depend on all of us to keep the space nice and tidy. There will be wardrobes. There will be a Lost & Found. Make use of them.


🎒 Packing list

Other useful items to bring are a head torch, ashtray for smokers, towels for sauna and playspace, as well as a camping chair if you wanna sit. But don’t worry, we will send you another reminder with a detailed packing list shortly before the event.


🎟️ Ticket resale

We are officially sold out! So if you cannot make it, another burner might gladly take your ticket off your hands. You can connect in our dedicated resale channel. You can initiate your own transfer through the ticket link you received until 24h hours before the event.
